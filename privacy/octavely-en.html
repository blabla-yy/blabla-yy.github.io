Octavely: Piano Practice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01 · 简体中文 · Terms of Use (Apple Standard EULA)

Octavely: Piano Practice ("the App") is a piano practice app for iPhone, iPad and Mac developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). It lets you practice with a MIDI keyboard (USB or Bluetooth) or the on-screen keyboard, read staff or numbered (jianpu) notation, play the built-in public-domain pieces, and import your own MusicXML, MXL or MIDI files. The App needs no account and keeps your data on your device. This policy explains what data the App processes and how.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your library, including score files you import. Imported files are stored inside the App's local database.
  • Practice records such as accuracy, timing and dates.
  • Your settings and preferences.

This data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and we have no servers that hold it.

2. MIDI Input, Microphone and Camera

Notes you play on a MIDI keyboard or the on-screen keyboard are processed on your device only, to evaluate your practice and play sound. They are never uploaded. The App does not use the microphone or the camera.

3. iCloud Sync (Optional, Pro)

Pro includes optional iCloud sync, which you control. When it is on, your library and practice records are synced through your own iCloud private database (CloudKit) so they are available on your other devices. The data goes to your iCloud account under Apple's terms; we cannot access it. If you turn sync off, the App keeps working with local data only.

4. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand which features are useful and to catch problems, the App sends a small number of anonymous events to Aptabase, a privacy-focused, open-source analytics service. Examples of events:

  • The App was launched.
  • An import succeeded or failed (only the file format, never the file name or content).
  • A practice session was completed (only the mode).
  • The paywall was shown or a purchase was completed.
  • A PDF was exported.
  • A MIDI keyboard was connected (only the connection type, such as USB or Bluetooth, never the device name).

Events contain no personal data such as your name, email address or Apple ID, and never contain file names, file contents or paths. According to Aptabase's privacy policy, its SDK does not collect any device-specific identifier (such as a device ID, hostname or hardware identifier), and it generates an anonymous user identifier on its server from a hash of the IP address and a salt that differs for each app and is discarded every 24 hours.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Share Usage Data".

5. In-App Purchase

Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your payment card number, Apple ID credentials or other payment information. The purchase supports Family Sharing.

6. Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, the App opens an email draft. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and it contains only what you write plus the App version, OS version and device model, which are shown in the email body before you send.

7. PDF Export

PDFs are generated on your device and saved to the location you choose. They are not uploaded anywhere by the App.

8. What We Do Not Do

  • No account or login is required.
  • We do not show ads or use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the App does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • We do not sell your data.

9. Imported Content

Files you import stay on your device (and in your own iCloud if you enable sync). We do not provide, host or review user content, and you are responsible for having the rights to the files you import. Use of the App is governed by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

10. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple (App Store, StoreKit and, if you enable sync, iCloud) and on Aptabase for the anonymous usage statistics in Section 4. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from any user.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the App changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
