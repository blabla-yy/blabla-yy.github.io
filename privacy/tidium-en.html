Tidium Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-09

Tidium ("the App") is a macOS file organizing tool developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). We built Tidium to be local-first: your files and their contents stay on your Mac. This policy explains what data the App processes and how.

1. We Do Not Upload Your Files

Tidium reads and organizes files only within folders you explicitly grant access to via the system file picker (macOS sandboxing). File content is analyzed on-device using Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models) or, where that is unavailable, a locally run model. We do not upload your file content, file names, or folder structure to our servers or to any third party.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

  • Folder bookmarks for locations you've chosen to organize.
  • Cached AI analysis results (e.g. suggested categories/names), keyed to file size and modification date, so files don't need to be re-analyzed unnecessarily.
  • Your custom organizing strategies and rule preferences.
  • Undo history for the current session, kept in memory only and lost when the App closes.

None of the above leaves your device.

3. Subscription and Purchase Data

  • Premium features are unlocked via an auto-renewable subscription purchased through the App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment card number or Apple ID credentials.
  • We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status and manage entitlements. RevenueCat receives subscription/transaction identifiers, product identifiers, and entitlement status from Apple in order to confirm your access to Premium features. RevenueCat and Apple process this information under their own privacy policies.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect or upload your file contents, file names, or folder structure.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not receive or store your full App Store payment information.

5. Your Choices

  • You control which folders the App can access, and can revoke access at any time via macOS System Settings.
  • You can clear cached AI analysis results and custom strategies from within the App.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID account settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Tidium relies on the Apple App Store for distribution and billing, and on RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management. These services may process data as described in their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Tidium does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since we do not collect personal data from any user in the course of normal operation, no additional measures are required.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the App's features change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
